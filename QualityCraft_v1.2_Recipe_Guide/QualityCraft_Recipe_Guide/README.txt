QUALITYCRAFT RECIPE GUIDE
Version: v1.2 for Minecraft Bedrock 26.50
Recipes indexed: 874

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Double-click QualityCraft_Recipe_Guide.html.
3. The guide opens in your normal web browser. No installation is required.

WHAT THE GUIDE DOES
- Searches all 874 QualityCraft recipes by result, ingredient, category, or ID.
- Groups 874 recipes into 347 output items so alternate crafting methods are easier to compare.
- Browses recipes by category/material, station, type, unlock rule, version status, and progress.
- Shows the exact Crafting Table, Workbench, Uncrafter, Stonecutter, or cooking station.
- Uses Minecraft-style Crafting Table, Furnace, and Stonecutter interfaces.
- Shows whether a recipe is immediately available or which item unlocks it.
- Displays shaped, shapeless, and cooking recipes.
- Alternates materials accepted through tags such as planks, logs, wool, and stone.
- Summarizes input-to-output efficiency for every method.
- Connects related crafting, stonecutting, cooking, and uncrafting paths.
- Provides Minecraft-style tooltips by mouse hover, keyboard focus, or mobile tap.
- Creates direct links to individual recipe methods and can copy them from the detail window.
- Identifies the 1 new and 5 updated recipes in version 1.2.
- Tracks recipes you mark as unlocked in the current browser.
- Exports and imports recipe progress as a JSON backup.

OFFLINE USE
The guide, recipe data, workstation interfaces, and artwork for all 644 items
referenced by the guide are embedded in the single HTML file and work offline.
No web server, installation, or internet connection is required.

IMPORTANT
This guide does not modify Minecraft or unlock recipes by itself. It explains the
trigger item and workstation required by the addon. Progress checkboxes are only a
personal checklist and do not read your Minecraft save.
