How do I unlock a recipe?
1. Find the resultSearch by the output, an ingredient, a recipe identifier, or a category such as “universal dyeing.”
2. Obtain the trigger itemThe gold line on each card shows the item that reveals the recipe. “Available immediately” needs no discovery item.
3. Use the listed stationOpen the Crafting Table, Workbench, Uncrafter, Stonecutter, or furnace-type station shown on the card.
