QUALITYCRAFT RECIPE GUIDE
Version: v1.1 for Minecraft Bedrock 26.50
Recipes indexed: 873

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Double-click QualityCraft_Recipe_Guide.html.
3. The guide opens in your normal web browser. No installation is required.

WHAT THE GUIDE DOES
- Searches all 873 QualityCraft recipes by result, ingredient, category, or ID.
- Shows the exact Crafting Table, Workbench, Uncrafter, Stonecutter, or cooking station.
- Shows whether a recipe is immediately available or which item unlocks it.
- Displays shaped, shapeless, and cooking recipes.
- Alternates materials accepted through tags such as planks, logs, wool, and stone.
- Tracks recipes you mark as unlocked in the current browser.
- Exports and imports recipe progress as a JSON backup.

OFFLINE USE
The guide itself and all recipe data work offline. Vanilla item artwork is loaded from
an online asset service when available. When offline, readable color-and-letter item
tiles are shown instead. QualityCraft's custom Workbench and Uncrafter artwork is
embedded and always available.

IMPORTANT
This guide does not modify Minecraft or unlock recipes by itself. It explains the
trigger item and workstation required by the addon. Progress checkboxes are only a
personal checklist and do not read your Minecraft save.
