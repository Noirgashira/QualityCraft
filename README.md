QualityCraft

QualityCraft is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition quality-of-life add-on that expands crafting, recycling, conversion, smelting, and Stonecutter options while staying close to vanilla Minecraft.

Instead of introducing an entirely new progression system, QualityCraft focuses on making existing materials more useful and crafting less tedious. It adds hundreds of carefully designed recipes, along with two custom crafting stations: the Workbench and Uncrafter.

What does QualityCraft add?

Expanded Crafting
QualityCraft adds alternative and convenient recipes for many vanilla items. Recipes are designed to feel like natural extensions of Minecraft rather than shortcuts that completely bypass normal resource gathering.

Workbench
The custom QualityCraft Workbench provides additional crafting recipes and conversions, including recipes that can accept groups of compatible materials such as different plank, log, wool, and stone types.

Uncrafter
Made too many stairs? Have slabs you don't need anymore? The QualityCraft Uncrafter lets you recycle many crafted items back into useful materials.

Examples include:

Stairs → source blocks/materials
Slabs → source blocks/materials
Doors and trapdoors → planks
Concrete stairs and slabs → concrete
Wool stairs and slabs → wool
Cushions → wool slabs
Many additional reversible material conversions

Recipes are balanced around the amount of material originally used whenever practical.

Expanded Stonecutter Support
The Stonecutter becomes useful for considerably more materials. QualityCraft provides additional conversion options for wood families and other suitable blocks, reducing repetitive crafting while keeping material costs predictable.

The Bedrock 26.50 version includes extensive Poplar support as well as convenient conversions involving the new wool building variants.

Better Material Compatibility
Where appropriate, QualityCraft uses Minecraft's material tags instead of unnecessarily restricting recipes to a single block variant. Compatible logs, planks, wool, and stone materials can therefore work across supported recipes.

More Crafting, Conversion & Smelting Options
QualityCraft also contains numerous smaller quality-of-life recipes for vanilla resources, building materials, storage, decorative blocks, conversions, recycling, and smelting.

Designed for Vanilla Survival

QualityCraft is intended to complement vanilla Minecraft rather than replace it. There are no classes, skill trees, complicated progression systems, or required scripting systems. The focus is simply:

More sensible recipes. More ways to use your materials. Less unnecessary crafting frustration.

Minecraft Bedrock Edition

QualityCraft is built specifically for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition and the current v1.1 release targets Bedrock 26.50 or newer.
